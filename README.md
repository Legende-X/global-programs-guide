## About This Guide

This guide accompanies the post *"The Quiet Decline of the Four-Year Degree"* on the Légende X Chronicles newsletter. It curates 18 of the strongest academic, credentialing, and apprenticeship programs around the world, mapped to fields most in demand by employers in 2026.

Filter programs by category:

- AI & Tech
- Business
- Health
- Skilled Trades
- Creative & Design

Each card shows program duration, cost, platform, employer recognition, and key skills covered.
